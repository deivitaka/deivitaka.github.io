Foldette

Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Foldette is a paper-folding puzzle game. It has no accounts, no purchases and no social features. This page explains what the app stores and what it sends.

Stored on your device

Your progress (completed levels, best fold counts, revealed hints), your settings (music, sound, vibration, language) and a small queue of analytics events are saved in the app's private storage on your device. None of this leaves the device except as described below.

Analytics (GameAnalytics)

To understand which levels are too hard or too easy, the app sends gameplay events to GameAnalytics: level started, completed or quit, and ad events. Each event carries a random identifier generated on first launch, plus your device model, operating system version, app version and a session counter. The identifier is not linked to your name, email or any advertising identifier.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Foldette shows an optional rewarded video when you ask for a hint, and may show other ads between levels. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use the device's advertising identifier and other device information to deliver and measure ads. You can limit this in your device settings: on iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising; on Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

What we don't do

We don't ask for an account, your name, email, location, contacts or photos. We don't sell your data.

Children

Foldette is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
